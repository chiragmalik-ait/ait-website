Every agent has a named owner, who receives its exceptions and approvals. Every run is logged with the evidence behind each decision, so you can replay what happened and why.

Models run in your cloud account, so data stays where it lives and nothing leaves your network. We build on AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud. We are ISO/IEC 27001 certified and HIPAA compliant, and BAAs are available. ISO/IEC 42001 and CMMC Level 3 are in progress.

Agents connect to Salesforce, Microsoft Teams, your ERP and your inbox over standard connectors like MCP, so there is nothing to replace.
